1. Text preprocessing :
Raw text
Lowercase     : .lower()
Tokenization  : converting text into smaller units called tokens 
Remove punctuation
Remove stopwords
Stemming       : Stemming tries to reduce words to a common root by cutting prefixes/suffixes.
Lemmatization : Lemmatization attempts to convert a word into its proper dictionary/base form.
Clean tokens

2.  Text representation : converting text into numbers so the ml model can understand that

a. bag of words : converts text into vectors based on occurence of words 
ignore the order , grammer , or relationship between words\
in th ematrix form the vetors are given to ml model 

Term frequency and inverse document frequency
b. TF and IDF :  it assign a numerical weight to word baesd on frequncy and how rare the word is
How important is a word to a particular document relative to the whole collection of documents?
gives higher imprtance to word that are frequent and uncommon
 TF = TF measures how frequently a term occurs within a document.
 #IDF : IDF measures how rare a word is across documents.

A word gets a high weight if it occurs frequently in a particular document but occurs in relatively few documents overall.

|                              | Bag of Words         | TF-IDF                   |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Representation               | Word counts          | Weighted word values     |
| Uses word frequency          | Yes                  | Yes                      |
| Considers document frequency | No                   | Yes                      |
| Common words                 | Can have high values | Usually get lower weight |
| Rare/discriminative words    | No special treatment | Higher weight            |
| Output                       | Numerical matrix     | Numerical matrix         |

c. word embeddings : dense numerical vector representation of words 
words with similar meaning(semantic properties) tend to have similar vecotr representation
