Association Rule Mining is an unsupervised data-mining technique used to discover relationships or co-occurrence patterns among items in a dataset.
commonly used in trasacntion data 
transcation : one observation containing a set of items 


3 important measures :
a. supoort: how frequent an items occur in the dataset
min_support = 0.05 , by giving this threshold , itemset which has low support can be removed from dataset

b. Confidence :  Given that \(X\) occurs, how often does \(Y\) also occur?
X= anticedent(before the condition , left side inuput)  Y= consequent (output , after condition)
Confidence measures the strength of a directional rule.

c. lift : Lift measures how much more frequently \(X\) and \(Y\) occur together compared with what would be expected if they were independent.

important algorithms :

1. Apriori : If an itemset is frequent, its subsets must also be frequent.
it progressively generates itemsets and remove the itemset that dont justify minimum support threshold

2.FP-Growth : Uses an FP-tree (Frequent Pattern Tree) to find frequent itemsets without generating as many candidate itemsets as Apriori.

workflow of association rule text mining : 
Transaction Dataset
        ↓
Find Frequent Itemsets
        ↓
Generate Association Rules
        ↓
Calculate Support
        ↓
Calculate Confidence
        ↓
Calculate Lift
        ↓
Select Interesting Rules
